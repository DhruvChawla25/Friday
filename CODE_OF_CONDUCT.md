# Code of Conduct

Friday follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Enforcement
Report unacceptable behaviour to **dhruvchawla2511@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or block contributors who break these rules.
